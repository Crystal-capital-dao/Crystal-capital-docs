# ⚖️ Legal Framework, Governance & Capital Security
### Crystal Capital Protocol | Compliance, Asset Isolation & Governance Standards

This document defines the structural legal framework, custody architecture, and investor protection standards governing Crystal Capital operations.

---

## 🏛 1. Institutional Governance & Structural Overview

* **Corporate Entity & Jurisdiction:** Operations are structured under an SPV (Special Purpose Vehicle) investment framework aligned with United Arab Emirates (UAE) international financial compliance guidelines.
* **DAO Hybrid Architecture:** Strategic capital allocation decisions and risk parameter updates are verified via multi-signature consensus among fund principals and quantitative directors.
* **High-Water Mark Policy:** Performance fees are strictly calculated on net new profits generated above the historical equity peak (High-Water Mark), preventing duplicate fee assessments after market drawdowns.

---

## 🔐 2. Custody & Non-Custodial Architecture

Crystal Capital implements a strict non-custodial SMA (Separately Managed Account) execution framework:

* **Zero Direct Custody Risk:** Investor capital remains directly in the client's verified institutional sub-accounts (Bybit, Hyperliquid, BingX).
* **Trade-Only API Permissions:** API connectivity is restricted exclusively to order placement and position management. All withdrawal, internal transfer, and lending permissions are permanently disabled.
* **IP Whitelisting:** All trading algorithms communicate with exchanges through dedicated, encrypted institutional server IP addresses.

---

## 🛡 3. Compliance & Risk Mitigation (KYC/AML)

* **Investor Mandate:** Capital allocation is restricted to accredited investors, institutional partners, Family Offices, and qualified Web3 syndicates.
* **Source of Funds Verification:** All participating allocations undergo standard Anti-Money Laundering (AML) checks and on-chain transaction provenance verification.
* **Capital Segregation:** RWA development assets and quantitative trading balances are completely isolated across distinct operational and smart contract pools.
