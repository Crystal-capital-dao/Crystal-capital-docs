# 🌱 Project C-Fusion: AgTech Infrastructure & RWA Tokenization
### United Arab Emirates | Industrial Gourmet Mushroom Production | Asset-Backed Yield

Project C-Fusion is an institutional Real-World Asset (RWA) project combining automated climate-controlled mushroom agriculture with tokenized cash-flow participation under Crystal Capital.

---

## 🏭 1. Operational Overview & Infrastructure

* **Facility Scope:** 1,200 m² base industrial production module located in the United Arab Emirates.
* **Specialization:** Controlled-environment cultivation of premium culinary and functional mushroom varieties (Shiitake, Lion's Mane, Oyster).
* **Automation:** Fully automated climate, humidity, and airflow control systems ensuring predictable harvest cycles and minimal labor overhead.
* **Local Market Advantage:** Import substitution addressing high local demand across UAE hospitality and retail sectors.

---

## 💎 2. Tokenized Economic Architecture (`cFUNGI`)

* **Asset-Backed Structure:** The `cFUNGI` protocol is 100% backed by physical facility assets, cultivation equipment, and real operational cash flows.
* **Target ROI:** **200%+** projected cumulative return across full project lifecycle.
* **Revenue Anchor:** Distribution tied directly to verified operational $EBITDA$ and secured off-take contracts.

---

## 💧 3. Investor Waterfall Distribution Model

To protect investor capital, distributions follow a prioritized waterfall framework:

1. **Stage 1 (Capital Recovery):** **80%** of net operational cash flow is distributed to investors until **100% of the initial principal** is fully recovered.
2. **Stage 2 (Passive Yield):** Post-recovery distribution permanently transitions to a **50/50** operational profit split between investors and the management protocol.
